Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026. This policy describes the iOS app Family House Points (House Points, bundle id com.boonewhite.housepoints), operated by Boone White.

Family House Points does not show ads, does not sell personal information, and does not use third-party tracking.

Who this covers

A parent creates the account and the child profiles. A child phone joins with a family code and uses an anonymous sign-in. The parent decides what is written about a child. The app does not ask children for an email address, phone number, photo library, contacts, or location.

Information the app stores

Account and family data is stored in Supabase so the family can sign in and sync.

Parent account Email address, password (handled by the authentication service), display name, and a user id. Used to sign in, recover a password, and keep the family together.
Family Family name, an eight-character invite code, and which accounts belong to the family as a parent or a child device.
Children Profiles the parent creates: name or nickname, point balance, avatar emoji, and color.
Family content Rewards and their point costs, point history and the notes a parent saves, and point or reward requests, including whether a parent approved or declined them.
Child device An anonymous user id for that phone, linked to the family after the code is entered. It is used to sync the family. It is not the child’s name or email.
Subscription Whether the family has House Points Premium, the product (monthly or yearly), renewal information, and the family id used to unlock every phone in that family. Apple charges the Apple ID. The app does not receive the card number.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, search history, or advertising identifiers. If a parent uses the iPhone keyboard microphone for a quick command, dictation is an iOS feature. The app receives the transcribed text. It does not receive or keep the audio. If that text is saved as a point reason, it is stored as family content.

On the device

The signed-in session and the local parent PIN are kept in encrypted storage on the phone. The parent PIN stays on that phone and is separate from the account password. The app can also keep a local copy of family data so a parent change can wait until the phone is online.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts authentication and the family database.
  • Apple processes the in-app purchase and provides the App Store subscription.
  • RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and the family id so House Points Premium can be confirmed for every phone in the family. RevenueCat is used for subscriptions, not for advertising.

These providers process data to run the app. Family information is not sold, and it is not used for third-party tracking or advertising.

How long it is kept

Family data is kept while the parent account and family exist. On the free plan, activity older than 30 days stays stored and is hidden in the app until the family has Premium.

When a parent deletes the account in the app, the family that parent created is deleted, including child profiles, rewards, requests, and point history, and the parent login is deleted. A record of the subscription event may remain without the family id so a purchase can still be reconciled. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Removing a child phone deletes that phone’s anonymous access. It does not delete the family’s points, rewards, or the parent account.

Account and data deletion

Parents can delete the account and family in the app: Settings → Delete account and family. Anyone can also email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com and ask Boone White to delete the account and its data. Include the parent email on the account.

Deleting the House Points account does not cancel House Points Premium. Cancel the subscription in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Children’s data

Child profiles are created and managed by the parent. The parent can remove a child from Settings, which also removes that child’s activity history. The parent can delete the whole family as described above. A child does not have a separate email account inside the app.
