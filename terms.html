The service

Family House Points lets a parent run a private points-and-rewards system for their family. A parent creates an account with email and password, creates one family, and adds child profiles. Child phones join with an eight-character family code. That code adds a read-only child device. It does not create another parent account. The same parent login can be used on the parent’s phones.

Parents give or take points and manage rewards. On House Points Premium, kids can request points or rewards, and parents approve or decline. Family data syncs through Supabase. The parent is responsible for the names, notes, and rewards they enter, and for who is given the family code.

Points and rewards

House Points are defined by the family. They have no cash value, are not money, and cannot be redeemed with Boone White or Apple. Rewards are whatever the family agrees to provide. Boone White does not supply those rewards.

House Points Premium

House Points Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription for the whole House Points family. A parent buys it once. Every parent phone signed into that account, and every child phone that joined that family, is included. It is purchased with Apple in-app purchase and managed by RevenueCat. It is not shared through Apple Family Sharing.

Accounts and deletion

You need a parent account to create a family. Keep the password and the device PIN private. You can reset the password from the sign-in screen.

A parent can permanently delete the account and the family they created in Settings → Delete account and family. That removes the child profiles, rewards, requests, and point history stored for that family, then deletes the parent login. A child phone can remove its own anonymous access from the lock button. That removal leaves the family’s points and rewards in place.
