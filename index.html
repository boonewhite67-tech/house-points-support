Family House Points

A private points-and-rewards app for parents and kids. The App Store name is House Points. The bundle id is com.boonewhite.housepoints.

What the app is

Parents keep a family score of everyday choices. They add or subtract points, write a short reason, and set up rewards the family can work toward. Kids see their balance and how close they are to the next reward.

On House Points Premium, a child can ask for points or a reward, and a parent approves or declines each request. Points are a family score. They have no cash value.

Parents

Create an account with email and password, name the family, add child profiles, award or deduct points, manage rewards, and review activity. A 4-digit PIN on the phone locks parent controls when the phone is in child mode.

Child phones

A child phone joins with the eight-character family code from parent Settings. That phone is read-only except for Premium requests. It does not get a parent account.

Across the house

Family changes sync between phones. A parent can record a change while offline, and the app sends it when the phone is online again. The same parent account can be signed in on the parent’s phones.

How a family starts

  1. On the parent phone, choose I’m a parent, create an account, and confirm the email if the app asks you to.
  2. Name the family. The app creates an eight-character family code.
  3. Add a child profile: a name, an avatar, and a color. The free plan includes one child. Children already in the family stay if you later use the free plan.
  4. On a child’s phone, choose Join my family as a child and enter the code. You can share the code from Settings, or replace it so the old code stops working.
  5. Give points from Home, or type a short phrase in Quick command, such as “10 points for Matthew.”

Free and Premium

Free

One child, points, rewards, and the last 30 days of history. Older activity stays saved and is hidden until the family has Premium.

House Points Premium

$2.99 per month or $19.99 per year in the United States, with a 1-week free trial. One parent subscription covers every phone in that House Points family: unlimited children, kid requests with parent approval, and full history.

Apple charges the parent’s Apple ID. The subscription renews until it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Restore a purchase from the Premium screen in the app. Full terms are on the Terms of Use page.

FAQ

Can I invite another parent with the family code?

The family code joins a child phone. It does not create a second parent login. Sign the parent account in with email and password on the parent’s phones. Each parent account belongs to one family.

What can a child phone do?

It shows that child’s points, rewards, and progress. With Premium, the child can request points or a reward. A parent still approves or declines. The lock button on a joined child phone can remove that phone’s access. Removing the phone does not delete the family’s points or rewards.

Where is the parent PIN?

Settings includes Change parent PIN. The PIN is four digits and stays on the device. It is the lock between child mode and parent controls on that phone. It is separate from the account password.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password? The app emails a reset link to the parent account.

How do I delete my account and family data?

On the parent phone, open Settings and choose Delete account and family. That permanently deletes the family that parent created, including child profiles, rewards, requests, and point history, and then deletes the parent account. You can also email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com and ask for the account and its data to be deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel that in iPhone Settings.

Does the app show ads or sell our information?

No. Family House Points has no ads, does not sell personal information, and does not use third-party tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
